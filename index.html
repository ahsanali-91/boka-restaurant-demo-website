Since the square was young

A family taverna in the heart of Tiranë

Boka sits on Sheshi Avni Rustemi the way it always has — grapevines overhead, long tables filling in by dusk, the smell of grilled meat and fresh byrek drifting out to the square. It's the kind of place locals bring visitors when they want the real thing, not a postcard of it.

Every dish on the menu is built from recipes that predate the restaurant itself — passed down, argued over, and perfected one dinner service at a time. Nothing is plated for a photograph; it's plated to be eaten with your hands, shared, and followed by another round of raki.

The terrace

Dinner happens outside, under the lights

Boka's covered terrace is the reason people linger past midnight — string lights strung through the pergola, umbrellas for the afternoon sun, and on the right night, live folk music and a floor that turns into a dance circle without much warning.

  • Vine-shaded seating for up to 120 guests
  • Live traditional music, Thursday–Saturday evenings
  • Heated terrace through the cooler months
  • Steps from Sheshi Avni Rustemi, easy to find, easy to return to
